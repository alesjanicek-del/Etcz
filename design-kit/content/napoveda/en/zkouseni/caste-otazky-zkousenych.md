# Test taker FAQ

What the messages mean when you enter a code, and what to do when something does not work.

## “This code is not valid.”

Check how you typed the code. Watch out for characters that look alike, such as 0 and O. Capital letters, spaces and hyphens do not matter.

If the code still does not work, ask your instructor for a new one.

## “The test for this code is not available now (it has not started yet or has already ended).”

The test is available only at a set time, or your instructor has already closed it. Check the date and time with the organiser.

## “You have entered the code too many times. Try again after …”

This protects codes from being guessed. Wait until the time shown and carefully enter the code again.

## “You have used all permitted attempts.” or “You have already passed this test. No further attempt is needed.”

The test allows only a limited number of attempts, or a retake only if you did not pass. Ask the organiser for another attempt.

## “The test password is incorrect.”

Your instructor gives you the test password. It is not the access code. The message shows how many more times you can enter it.

## “The test cannot be started now. Contact your instructor.”

The test could not be prepared, usually because of an error in the questions. It is not your fault. Contact your instructor.

## Closed window, lost connection, flat battery

Your saved answers are not lost, but the timer keeps running. The steps are in [Lost connection, closed window, another device](vypadek-a-jine-zarizeni.md). Your instructor can give you extra time.

## At a supervised in-room exam, I see “You have been removed from the waiting room”

Your instructor removed you from the waiting room, for example by mistake. Speak to them and join again.

## Does the app record what I do?

Yes, but only in the test window: starting, viewing questions, changing answers, leaving the test window and submitting. The app does not monitor your camera, microphone or other programs. The record is there to document how the exam went. Details are in the privacy notice; the link is on the test start page.

## Can I open help during the test?

Yes. Below the test name, expand **How the test works**. Do not open other windows; leaving the window is recorded.

## Where can I find my result?

See [Submission and result](odevzdani-a-vysledek.md).
