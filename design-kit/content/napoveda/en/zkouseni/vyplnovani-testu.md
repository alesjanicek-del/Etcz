# Taking the test

Time left, question overview, flagging for review, one or more correct answers, and saving.

Every test taker gets their own test version: a different selection and order of questions and answers. So do not compare question numbers with your neighbour.

## Top bar

At the top, you see **Time left** and the save status (“Saved hh:mm:ss”). The app alerts you every 10 minutes of remaining time (for example, 30, 20 and 10 minutes before the end), at half time, and 5 minutes and 1 minute before the end.

## Question overview

The numbers at the top are the question overview:

- ✓ answered question,
- ⚑ question you want to come back to.

You can click any number.

## Answering

- A question with more than one correct answer says “(more than one correct answer)” in its heading. Without this note, the question has one correct answer.
- For a question with more than one correct answer, select all the correct answers and no incorrect ones.
- Tick **Flag this question for review** to mark a question.

Depending on the test settings, you see:

- one question per page, with the buttons **← Previous**, **Next question →** and **Save and review**,
- or all questions at once, with a **Save** button.

Practice tests may have a **Confirm answer** button. Once you confirm, the answer is locked and you see straight away whether it was correct.

## Saving

Answers are saved automatically after every change. The save buttons are just a backup.

If an answer cannot be saved, you see a message and a **Try saving again** button. The app also retries by itself.

## Help during the test

Below the test name is an expandable **How the test works** section with a short summary. It opens right on the page, so you do not need to go anywhere.

Do not open other windows or apps during the test. Leaving the test window is recorded and your instructor will see it.

## Keyboard use and screen reader labels

You can use the test with a keyboard only (Tab, Space, arrow keys), and elements are labelled for screen readers (we are preparing a manual test with a screen reader). It also works when zoomed up to 400%. Details are in [Accessibility for test takers](pristupnost.md).
