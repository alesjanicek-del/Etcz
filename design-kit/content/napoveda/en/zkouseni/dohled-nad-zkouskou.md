# Exam monitoring

What monitoring means, what is recorded, the warning after leaving the window, and full screen.

For some tests, the organiser turns on exam monitoring. You will know in advance from the test start page, which also says exactly what is recorded. During the test, a reminder appears above the questions.

## What is recorded

- when you leave the test window or tab and when you return (switching to another window, tab or app; on a phone, also turning the screen off),
- leaving full screen, if the test offers it,
- copying question text and pasting from the clipboard,
- for some tests, a first answer to a question that comes unusually quickly after it appears.

Your camera image, sound, screen content and other apps are not recorded. Monitoring does not use artificial intelligence. Your instructor sees the record, and a person decides what it means.

## Warning after leaving the window

If you leave the test window and come back, a warning may appear above the questions: “You left the test window. This has been recorded.” The warning does not block anything. Close it with **OK, continue** and carry on with the test. Screen readers read it out, and the focus stays where it was.

Some tests submit automatically after you leave the window a set number of times. The start page and the text above the questions say after how many times this happens. A counter shows how many times you have already left the window. The answers saved up to that moment are submitted. The result page shows why the test was submitted.

To avoid leaving the window by mistake: close other apps beforehand, turn off notifications and do not let your phone screen turn off. The **How the test works** help on the test page does not leave the window.

## Full screen

If the test offers full screen, click **Go full screen**. On the next question, full screen comes back the first time you click or press a key. If you leave it (for example, by pressing Esc), this is recorded and the test offers you a way back.

If your browser, phone or assistive technology does not support full screen, take the test as normal. Nothing is recorded against you for this.

## Copying

If copying is blocked, you cannot copy question text or paste text into answers. Selecting text, zooming the page and screen readers are not restricted.

## Disabilities and health conditions

If you use assistive technology that takes over the window (such as dictation or a separate magnifier), tell your instructor beforehand. Your instructor can choose recording only, without automatic submission, or a test without monitoring. You do not have to give a diagnosis. Extra time works with monitoring too.

## Disagree with automatic submission?

Automatic submission after leaving the window repeatedly was decided by software, not a person. You have the right to give your view and to ask a person at the exam organiser to review the submission and the result. Write to them as soon as possible after the exam and explain why you left the window. The organiser can keep the result or allow you a new attempt. You will find the organiser’s contact details and more information in the personal data processing information, under “Exam monitoring” (linked in the footer of the test page).
