# Lost connection, closed window, another device

What to do if your internet drops, you close the window by mistake or your phone battery dies.

## Internet outage

A yellow bar appears: “You are offline – your answers will be saved when the connection is back. The timer keeps running.”

Carry on answering. When the connection is back, your answers are saved automatically. Check that the status has changed to “Saved…”.

## You closed the window by mistake

Your saved answers are not lost. But the timer keeps running.

- **With a code:** enter the same code again on the code entry page. You continue where you left off.
- **With a student account:** in **My tests**, click **Continue**.
- **With a link or at a supervised in-room exam:** open the test page again in the same browser, for example from your history. If that does not work, speak to your instructor.

## Continuing on another device

You can take the test on only one device at a time. If you open it somewhere else, you see **The test is open on another device**.

1. Ask your instructor for a handover code. It is valid for 10 minutes.
2. On the new device, enter it in the **Handover code** field. On the code entry page, use the **Handover code from your instructor** field.
3. Click **Continue on this device**.

The test is blocked on the original device. The timer keeps running in the meantime.

## Your instructor can give you extra time

Did you lose time because of an outage? Tell your instructor. They can give you extra time and record the reason.

*For roles: Test taker, Student*
