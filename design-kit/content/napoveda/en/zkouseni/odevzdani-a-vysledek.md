# Submission and result

How to submit the test, confirm the training, what happens when time runs out and what you see after submitting (result, result report, certificate).

## Submitting

1. Click **Review and submit**. On the last question (when questions are shown one at a time), the button is **Save and review** instead.
2. A summary appears: how many questions you have answered, which are unanswered and which you flagged for review. The question numbers link back to the questions. Click **Back to questions** to return.
3. Click **Submit test** and confirm.

Once you submit, you can no longer change your answers.

When time runs out, the app submits the test automatically. All answers saved before the time limit ended are kept.

## Confirmation of training

For some training (for example, health and safety), your organisation needs proof that you have been made aware of the training content. On the summary before submitting, you then see a **Confirmation of training** box with the training name, the trainer and the syllabus.

1. Read the syllabus.
2. Tick “I confirm that I have read and understood the training content…”.
3. Click **Submit test**.

Confirm only what is true. If you do not understand the training content, ask your instructor before submitting. You cannot submit the test yourself without ticking the box. The confirmation is stored with the time and your name in the exam record, and your organisation sees it in the training record. If time runs out or your instructor ends the test, it is submitted without the confirmation.

## What you see after submitting

The organiser decides what is shown:

- only the confirmation **Test submitted** and “The exam organiser will tell you your result”,
- your result as a percentage and points, and **Passed** or **Not passed**, or a grade,
- also the questions you answered incorrectly, or a full breakdown with the correct answers.

Correct answers are never shown for official exams.

The organiser can also release results later, once they have evaluated the exam.

## Result report

If the organiser shows your result, you can download your result report with **Download result report (PDF)**. Otherwise, the organiser gives you a copy of the report.

The downloaded report is a **test taker’s copy**: it includes questions only as far as the test shows them (for example, only incorrectly answered questions, or none). This is stated in the header. The organiser keeps the full report with all questions. Both have the same verification code. The report also shows the time limit and any extra time.

## Certificate

If the test issues a certificate and you pass, your result shows the training name, the date you passed and the date the certificate is valid until. Download it with **Download certificate (PDF)**.

- The certificate has the same verification code and QR code as the result report. Anyone who knows the code can check on the verification page that the certificate is genuine and still valid. So only show the code to people you want to prove your result to.
- The “valid until” date is stored when you submit. Later changes to the test do not affect it.
- Your organisation issues the certificate. It is not an official document issued by a public authority.
- Before the certificate expires, your organisation may send you an email reminder (only if you have an account or received your code by email). If you do not want reminders, contact your organisation.

## Learners with an account

You can find your results at any time under **My results** in the menu. Until your instructor releases the result, the test shows “not yet released”. Download the result report with **Result report (PDF)** and the certificate with **Certificate (PDF)**. For certificates, you also see the expiry date and the status (valid, expired).

## Test takers without an account

You see your result, result report and certificate on the page shown after you submit. You can return to them later by entering your code again on the **Enter test** page, if the organiser allows it. If your instructor releases results later, the organiser will tell you your result.

*For roles: Test taker, Learner*
