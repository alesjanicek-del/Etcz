# Accessibility for test takers

Keyboard use, screen reader labels, text zoom and extra time for people with disabilities or health conditions.

## Controls

- You can use the test with a keyboard only: Tab moves between elements, Space ticks an answer and Enter activates a button.
- Elements are labelled for screen readers, but we are still preparing a manual test with a screen reader.
- Question authors can add screen reader descriptions to images and formulas. If one is missing, ask your instructor for help.
- You can zoom the page up to 400% (Ctrl and +). The content reflows and nothing is lost.
- On a phone, questions fit the screen width.

## Extra time

Do you need more time because of a disability or health condition? Ask your instructor or the organiser before you start. Extra time is set on your code or account (25%, 50% or 100%). Your instructor can also add time during the test.

## Accessibility statement

You will find details, and a contact for reporting accessibility problems, in the **Accessibility statement** in the footer of every page.

*For roles: Test taker, Student*
