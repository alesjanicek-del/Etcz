# Signing in, password and signing out

How to sign in for the first time, what to do if you forget your password, and how to sign out on all devices.

## Signing in

1. Open your organisation’s app address. Your administrator will give it to you.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. If two-step verification is on, also enter the code from your phone. The steps are in [Two-step verification](overeni-ve-dvou-krocich.md).

Test takers without an account do not sign in. They open the test with a code, a link or at a supervised in-room exam. The steps are in [How to get to your test](../zkouseni/jak-se-dostat-k-testu.md).

## Signing in with an email link

If your organisation allows it, there is an **Email me a sign-in link** option below the sign-in form. You then do not need a password.

1. Click **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Enter your email and click **Send sign-in link**.
3. Open the link in the email and click **Sign in**.
4. If two-step verification is on, also enter the code from your phone. The link replaces your password, not two-step verification.

- The link is valid for 15 minutes and works only once. If it expires, request a new one.
- The app always gives the same reply, even if no account uses that email. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and that the address is correct.
- The link stops working if you change your password, sign out on all devices or sign in again.
- Ignore any link you did not request. Without it, nobody can sign in to your account.

How to turn this on for your organisation is described in [Signing in with an email link](../admin-organizace/prihlaseni-odkazem.md).

## First sign-in from an invitation

Your administrator creates your account. You receive an invitation email with a link where you set your own password.

- The link is valid for 7 days. If it expires, ask your administrator for a new invitation.
- Your password must have at least 12 characters. The app will not accept passwords that are too common.
- A sentence or a few unrelated words work well.

Nobody else knows your password, not even your administrator.

## Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgotten password**.
2. Enter your email and click **Send link**.
3. Open the link in the email and set a new password.

If the link has expired, you will see the **Link no longer valid** page. Click **Request a new link**.

No email? Check your spam folder.

## Changing your password

1. Open **My account**.
2. Click **Change password**.

You stay signed in on this device. The app signs you out on all other devices.

## Signing out

The **Sign out** button is at the top right.

Forgot to sign out on someone else’s computer? In **My account**, click **Sign out on all devices**.

## Why did the app sign me out?

The app signs you out when:

- you have been inactive for a while (administrators, authors and instructors after 30 minutes, students after 60 minutes),
- 12 hours have passed since you signed in (applies to everyone),
- you changed your password or an administrator changed your email,
- an administrator deactivated your account or reset your two-step verification,
- you used **Sign out on all devices**.

## Warning before you are signed out

2 minutes before you are signed out for inactivity, a **You will be signed out soon** window appears with a countdown. Click **Stay signed in** or press Esc to stay signed in and carry on working.

The 12-hour limit cannot be extended. The window also warns you 2 minutes before it; save your work and sign in again. After you sign in, you return to the page you were on.

The app keeps saving a question you are writing in the editor to the browser tab. You can restore it after you sign in again; see [Creating a question](../autor/vytvoreni-otazky.md).

After you click **Sign out**, the sign-in page shows “You have been signed out.” On a shared computer (such as a classroom), this confirms that signing out worked.

*For roles: Student, Question author, Instructor, Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
