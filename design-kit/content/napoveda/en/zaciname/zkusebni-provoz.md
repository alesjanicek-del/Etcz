# Getting started with a free trial

Registering your organisation, verifying your email, mandatory two-step verification and your first steps during 30 days free.

## Registration

On the app’s website, click **Create an organisation for free**. The button appears only when registration is open. Otherwise, **Book a guided demo** appears in its place (see below).

1. Fill in **Organisation name**.
2. Choose your **Organisation address**. Your organisation will be at this address, for example `name.app-address.cz`.
3. Fill in **Company ID (IČO)**, **Your full name**, **Work email** and **Password**. Your password must have at least 12 characters.
4. Accept the terms and conditions and click **Create organisation**.
5. The **Check your email** page appears. Click the link in the email. The link is valid for 48 hours.

Once your email is verified, the free trial starts and the app takes you to sign in to your organisation. You become the organisation administrator.

Some addresses need manual approval, for example if they look like the name of a public authority. You will then see **Email verified, registration pending approval**. Approval usually takes up to one working day, and you will get an email when it is done.

## What the free trial includes

- 30 days free, no payment card needed,
- 100 attempts, 1 branch and 3 staff users (administrators, instructors and question authors combined, including you),
- the same features as the paid Start plan.

You can see how much is left on the **Plan and usage** page. You then order a plan (see [Plan, orders and invoices](../admin-organizace/tarif-a-faktury.md)). If the trial ends without an order, your organisation becomes read-only. You do not lose your data.

## First sign-in: two-step verification

Organisation administrators must have two-step verification turned on. Straight after your first sign-in, the app takes you to set it up. Have your phone ready; it takes about 2 minutes. The steps are in [Two-step verification](overeni-ve-dvou-krocich.md).

## Your first five steps

1. Create a branch and invite colleagues: **Administration → Branches** and **Users** (see [Branches](../admin-organizace/pobocky.md) and [Users and invitations](../admin-pobocky/uzivatele-a-pozvanky.md)).
2. Create a question bank and add questions, or import them from an XLSX file (see [Question banks](../autor/soubory-otazek.md)).
3. Build a test (see [Building a test step by step](../lektor/sestaveni-testu.md)).
4. Try the test yourself with **Trial run**. This attempt does not count towards your limit.
5. Start testing: codes, link, assigned to a group or supervised in-room exam (see [Ways to assign a test](../lektor/zpusoby-zadani-testu.md)).

On the **Overview** page, the **Getting started** guide takes you through these steps. If you hid the guide, reopen it with **Show the first steps guide**.

## Demo without registration

If the website shows the **Try the demo** button, you can try the app in a ready-made sample organisation without registering or a password. Emails from the demo are in **Demo mailbox** in the menu. Demo data is deleted every night. Details are in [Public demo without registration](verejne-demo.md).

## Book a guided demo

Are you a public authority, or do you need a contract and individual terms? On the website, click **Book a guided demo**, enter your contact details and the operator will get in touch.

*For roles: Organisation administrator*
