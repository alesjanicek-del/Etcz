# Two-step verification (2FA)

Why you must set up two-step verification, how to turn it on and what to do if you lose your phone.

## Why do I have to set it up?

Two-step verification protects your account if someone gets hold of your password. As well as your password, you enter a six-digit code from an app on your phone. The code changes every 30 seconds.

The app holds official records and test takers’ personal data. That is why verification is mandatory for:

- organisation administrators, branch administrators and question authors, always,
- instructors who own or can edit a question bank or test, always,
- other instructors, if the organisation turns it on (**Require two-step verification for instructors too**).

Verification is not mandatory for students.

If verification is mandatory for your role, the app takes you to set it up straight after you sign in. You will see the message “Two-step verification is mandatory for your role.” Until you set it up, you cannot open any pages except help.

Setting it up takes about 2 minutes. You will need your phone.

## How to turn it on

1. Open **My account** and click **Turn on two-step verification**.
2. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, FreeOTP or Aegis.
3. In the authenticator app, scan the QR code on the screen. If that does not work, type the key in manually (type “time-based”, 6 digits, 30 s).
4. In the **Code from the app** field, type the six-digit code from your phone.
5. Click **Verify and turn on**.
6. Your **Backup codes** appear. Print them or save them in a password manager. They are shown only once.
7. Click **I’ve saved them, continue**.

You can use each backup code once instead of the code from your phone.

## Signing in with verification

After entering your email and password, fill in the **Code from authenticator app (or backup code)** field and click **Verify**.

## New backup codes

1. Open **My account** and click **Backup codes**.
2. Enter your **Current password**.
3. Click **Generate new codes**.

Your previous backup codes stop working.

## I’ve lost my phone

- **You have a backup code:** sign in with it. Then, in **My account**, click **Set up verification again** and set it up on your new phone.
- **You have no backup codes:** ask your organisation or branch administrator to reset your verification. The administrator will first confirm your identity, for example by phone. You set up verification again the next time you sign in. The steps for administrators are in [Resetting two-step verification](../admin-pobocky/zruseni-overeni.md).

*For roles: Student, Question author, Instructor, Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
