# Public demo without registration

What the public demo is, how to start it, what you can try and what happens to the data.

## What is the public demo?

The public demo is a sample organisation with fictional questions, tests and results. You can try the app there without registering, without a password and without an email address. Each visitor gets their own sample branch (for example, “Visitor 12”) that other visitors cannot see.

The demo is available only when the website shows the **Try the demo** button. Want the app for your organisation? Start a free trial (see [Getting started with a free trial](zkusebni-provoz.md)) or book a guided demo.

## How to start the demo

1. On the website, click **Try the demo**.
2. Choose a role: instructor, question author, branch administrator or test taker. **Instructor and test taker side by side** opens a supervised in-room exam in two windows. You can see how the test taker’s answers appear in the instructor’s live monitor.
3. Click **Start demo**. A few seconds later, you are signed in to your sample branch.

You can start the demo up to 5 times an hour from one connection. If too many visitors start the demo in one day, new starts are paused until the next day.

## What you can try in the demo

At the top of the page is a yellow demo bar. It contains:

- **Switch role**: changes your role in the same branch, and the data stays,
- **Demo inbox**: emails the app would send (invitations, access codes, results); the demo never sends any email,
- **Book a guided demo** and **I want the app for my organisation**.

In the menu, **Demo inbox** replaces **Support**. Some parts of the app are not in the demo: organisation settings, plan, branches, support requests, question bank import, organisation data export, account password and two-step verification. Instead, you see a “Not available in the demo” page. Branch content in the demo has upper limits, for example on the number of questions, tests and PDF downloads per hour.

## What happens to the data

- Demo data is fictional and is deleted every night at 03:00, together with your changes. The next day, you start with a new sample branch.
- Do not enter personal or real data in the demo, such as names, emails or questions from your exams.
- If you take a test as a test taker in the demo, your IP address and browser type are stored in the attempt log, just as in live use. They are deleted with the whole demo overnight.

## Returning to a demo in progress

Until the nightly deletion, your browser remembers your sample branch and last role (a strictly necessary cookie). If you return to the demo in the same browser, even after signing out, the app offers a **Continue the demo** button. In a different browser, you start with a new branch.

You do not sign in to the demo with a password or an email link. If the cookie is missing, click **Choose a role and start the demo**.

*For roles: everyone*
