A permanent record of important changes and downloads. Who did what, when, and why.

Open **Administration** → **Change log**.

## What the change log contains

It records every important change: who made it, when, what action, on which record and why. For example:

- downloads of result reports and print files,
- extra time,
- resets of two-step verification,
- question approvals,
- anonymisation and record protection.

Entries cannot be edited or deleted manually. The app deletes them automatically after 60 months (see Personal data, anonymisation and data export<!-- link: chapter URL not in crawl -->).

## Filter

Filter by **Action**, **From** and **To**.

- Branch administrators see actions by users in their own branches. The **Action** list only shows actions that appear in their entries.
- Organisation administrators see the whole organisation.

Actions taken by a support technician are labelled “Abuco support technician”.

*For roles: Branch administrator, Organisation administrator, Customer (authorised representative)*
