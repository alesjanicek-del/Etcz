Groups make it easy to assign a test to several learners at once.

## New group

1. Open **Administration** → **Learner groups**.
2. Click **New group**.
3. Enter a name and choose a branch.
4. Click **Create group**.

## Members

In the group details, go to **Members**, tick the learners and click **Save**. The list shows the branch’s active learners.

Membership takes effect immediately. A new member also sees tests already assigned to the group in **My tests**.

## Deleting a group

You can delete a group at any time with **Delete**. The learners stay in the system.

## Assigning a test to a group

When creating a test assignment, the instructor chooses **Assigned to learners / a group** and selects the group (see [Ways to assign a test](../lektor/zpusoby-zadani-testu.md)).

*For roles: Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
