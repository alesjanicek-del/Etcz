See all authors, give or remove the author role, and transfer question banks when an author leaves.

Open **Administration** → **Question authors**. For each author, the overview shows the number of questions, the last change, the number of question banks they own and their status.

## Add the author role

Select an instructor and click **Add author role**. To add a new instructor, invite them first on the **Users** page.

## Remove the role

Next to the author, click **Remove role**. Their authorship of questions stays in the history.

## Transferring question bank ownership

When an author leaves, transfer their question banks to another author:

1. In **Transfer question bank ownership**, choose the **Current owner** and the **New owner**.
2. Fill in a **Reason**.
3. Click **Transfer**.

The transfer is recorded in the change log.

*For roles: Organisation administrator*
