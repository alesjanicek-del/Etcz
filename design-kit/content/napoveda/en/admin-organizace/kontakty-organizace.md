Technical and commercial contacts, data protection officer and the customer’s authorised representative.

The app operator and the app use your organisation contacts:

- The **Customer’s authorised representative** can submit support requests if their contact is linked to an app account (see Reporting a fault and requesting support<!-- link: chapter URL not in crawl -->).
- The **Data protection officer** is shown to test takers in the privacy notice. If none is set, the app warns you.
- The operator uses the **Technical contact** and **Commercial contact** for operational and contractual matters.

## New contact

1. Open **Organisation contacts** (the link is in **Organisation settings**).
2. Click **New contact**.
3. Choose a **Contact role** and fill in the name, email and phone. For an authorised representative, select their user account in **App account (optional)**. Otherwise they cannot submit requests in the app. Only administrator, instructor and question author accounts are offered, not learner accounts.
4. Save.

You can add several contacts, even with the same role.

*For roles: Organisation administrator, Customer (authorised representative)*
