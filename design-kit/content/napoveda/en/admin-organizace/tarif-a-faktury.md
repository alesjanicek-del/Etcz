Your current plan and attempt usage, ordering a plan with a proforma invoice, and what happens when you reach the limit.

Open **Administration** → **Plan and usage**.

## What the page shows

- **Current plan**, **Status** (for example trial or active) and **Paid until**,
- **Attempt usage** for the period,
- the plan’s **Limits and features**.

## What counts as an attempt

An attempt is every test version created: each online test started and each printed test version. Trial runs do not count.

Once you reach the limit, you can use 10% more. The limit never interrupts an exam in progress.

## Ordering a plan

1. In **Order a plan**, select a plan.
2. Fill in the billing details: customer name, company ID, VAT ID, billing address and the email for the proforma invoice.
3. Click **Order and issue proforma invoice**.

The proforma invoice arrives by email. You can also find it in **Orders**. It is due within 14 days and has a QR code for payment. Once your payment arrives, the operator activates the plan for 12 months. The tax invoice arrives within 15 days of payment.

## Register of Contracts

This applies to bodies covered by Act No. 340/2015 Coll., on the Register of Contracts. Examples are municipalities, regions, schools and state-funded organisations. It matters if the plan costs more than CZK 50,000 a year excluding VAT, as the Síť (Network) plan does. You must then publish the order in the Register of Contracts within 30 days of concluding it. The contract takes effect only when published. If you do not publish it within 3 months, it is void from the start. This obligation is yours, not the operator’s (Article 11.3 of the Terms and Conditions). The app reminds you of it when you order and in the email with the proforma invoice. Publish the order together with the proforma invoice that confirms it. Payment activates the plan. If you cannot publish the contract before then, pay only after publishing it.

## Public administration and contracts

The Veřejná správa (Public Administration) plan is agreed by contract and cannot be ordered in the app. Abuco sales handles any changes.

## Expired or suspended subscription

Your organisation then becomes read-only. You cannot start new attempts or printing. Exams in progress can be finished. Your data is kept.
