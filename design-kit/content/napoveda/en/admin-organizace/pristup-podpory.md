A support technician can enter your organisation only with your permission. Everything they do is recorded in the change log.

## Support technician access

An Abuco support technician can enter your organisation only with your permission. This helps when they need to see a problem directly in your organisation.

1. Open **Administration** → **Organisation settings**, section **Support access**.
2. In **Abuco support technician access to the organisation**, choose:
   - **Off**: the technician cannot enter,
   - **On request (time window)**: in **Allow access for (hours)**, enter how many hours from now access stays open. Enter 0 to close the window immediately,
   - **Allowed**: the technician can enter at any time.
3. Click **Save settings**.

## What you will see

- Administrators receive an email when the technician enters.
- A red bar appears at the top while the technician is present. It says “Support technician signed in … – all actions are recorded in the organisation’s change log”.
- All their actions are labelled in the change log.

*For roles: Organisation administrator, Customer (authorised representative)*
