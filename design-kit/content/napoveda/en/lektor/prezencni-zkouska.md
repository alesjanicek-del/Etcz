An exam in the room without accounts: projector screen with a code and QR code, waiting room, locking entry and starting the test.

## Steps

1. Next to the test, select **Assign**, choose **Supervised in-room exam** and select **Create**.
2. In the assignment details, select **Projector screen ↗**. A new window opens for the projector. It shows a large code, a QR code and the address, but no participant names. The screen keeps showing how many people have joined, are writing and have submitted.
3. Participants scan the QR code or type in the code, then enter their name. They appear in the live monitor under **In the waiting room**.
4. Has someone joined who should not be there? Remove them with **✕**.
5. When everyone has joined, select **Lock entry and start test**. Each participant gets their first question within a few seconds. The button stays inactive until at least one person has joined.

Anyone who arrives late sees that entry is closed.

## Participant and test taker

A participant in a supervised in-room exam is a person in the room who joined with the code from the projector screen. Once the test starts, each participant has their own attempt, result and result report, like any other test taker. That is why the live monitor, results and result report refer to test takers.

## Two participants with the same name

The application does not let a second participant with the same name into the waiting room. Usually it is the same person on a second device. But there may really be two people with the same name in the room. The name a participant enters also appears in the result report. So agree in advance how to tell them apart consistently:

- For an official exam (for example, a special professional competence exam), ask the second participant to add an identifier after their name. Use one that the organisation also keeps on file, such as year of birth: “Jan Novák (1985)”. Do not use random numbers, which would mean nothing in the result report.
- Write the identifier on the exam attendance sheet.

## Tips for the room

- Hand out or project the **Instructions for test takers**. A one-page printable PDF is on the [help home page](../index.md), under “Downloads and printables”.
- Check that the Wi-Fi works and there are power sockets in the room.
- Tell participants not to switch to other apps during the test. Leaving the window is recorded, and you see it in the live monitor.
- Follow progress in the live monitor (see [Live monitor](zivy-panel.md)).
