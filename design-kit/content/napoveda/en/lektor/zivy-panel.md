Follow an exam in real time. The status of each test taker, time remaining and alerts when someone leaves the window.

Each assignment’s details include the **Live monitor**. It refreshes itself every 5 seconds. On the **Overview** page, there is a **Live monitor** link next to every exam in progress.

## What the monitor shows

Above the table is a summary: how many test takers are writing, how many have submitted and how many have passed.

For each test taker:

- **Status**: started, not opened yet, writing, submitted, or timed out,
- **Answered**: how many questions out of the total,
- **Remaining**: time remaining (“—” once submitted),
- **Result**: for submitted attempts, the percentage and Passed or Not passed,
- **Last activity**,
- **Alerts**: “⚠ left the window N×” (the test taker switched to another window or app); “↔ other device” (the test was handed over to another device); “disconnected N×”. If monitoring is on for the test, there is also a “Monitoring: …” badge with the number and type of events, and “Submitted by monitoring”. See [Exam monitoring](dohled-nad-zkouskou.md).

## Auto-refresh

The monitor refreshes itself every 5 seconds. To stop it, select **Pause auto-refresh**. For example, you may want to read the data calmly or you may be using a screen reader. The button changes to **Resume auto-refresh**. While paused, you can still load the current status with **Refresh now**. Your browser remembers the choice, even after you reload the page.

## How to read alerts

Leaving the window does not in itself mean cheating. It may be a phone notification or a key pressed by mistake. If an alert concerns you, ask the test taker and follow the exam rules. You can find the full activity record in the result details.

## Actions for a test taker

For an attempt in progress, select **Actions**. You can extend the time, allow handover to another device or end the attempt. The steps are in [Extending time, handover and ending an attempt](prodlouzeni-prevzeti-ukonceni.md).
