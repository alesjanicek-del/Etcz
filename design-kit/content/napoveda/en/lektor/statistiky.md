How many people took the test, pass rate, ranking of people and question statistics by version.

Open **Evaluation → Statistics**. Trial runs are not counted.

## Tests

Choose a test and, if you like, a single test assignment. You see:

- **People** and **Attempts**,
- **Average**, **Median** and **Passed attempts**,
- **Score distribution** in 10% bands,
- **Ranking**.

## Question banks

For each question, you see how many times it was:

- **Included** in test versions,
- **Shown (attempts)** and **Total views**,
- answered correctly (**Correct / evaluated**), and its **Success rate**.

The expandable **By version** section shows figures for each version and branch. The warning “Outdated version used” means an older version of the question was still appearing in exams.

## Question details

The details show figures by version. Under **Answer choices**, they show how many times test takers chose each answer. This helps you spot confusing or overly easy answers.

## Data protection

Figures from branches outside your access with fewer than 5 records are shown as “< 5”.

*For roles: Instructor, Question author, Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
