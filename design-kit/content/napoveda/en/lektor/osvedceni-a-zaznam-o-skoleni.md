A certificate with a validity period and QR code after passing the test, trainee confirmation before submitting, and a training record in PDF and XLSX.

When a test taker passes, the test can issue a certificate with an expiry date. Before they submit, it can require trainee confirmation. From the results, you can then download a training record with a list of the people trained. This is mainly useful for health and safety, fire safety or cyber security training.

## Test settings

Open the test → **3. Parameters** → the **Certificate and training record** section. More fields appear once you turn on certificates or confirmation:

- **Issue certificate on pass**: anyone who passes the test gets a PDF certificate.
- **Certificate validity (months)**: for example 12 or 24. Leave empty for a certificate with no expiry. A 24-month validity for a test passed on 28 Oct 2026 means “valid until 27 Oct 2028”.
- **Training name**: shown on the certificate and in the training record. If you leave it empty, the test name is used.
- **Trainer**: the trainer’s name. If you leave it empty, the instructor who created the test assignment is shown.
- **Require trainee confirmation**: before submitting, the test taker ticks a box to confirm they have been briefed on the training.
- **Training syllabus**: the text the test taker was briefed on. It is shown with the confirmation and in the training record.

You can change these settings even after test takers have taken the test for real. The change applies to attempts submitted after you save. Certificates already issued and confirmations already saved stay unchanged, because they are stored in the exam record on submission.

An official test does not issue certificates. The proof of an official exam is the result report.

## Certificate

- It is created only for a passed attempt with the test taker’s name. An anonymous attempt from a link without a name does not get a certificate.
- It contains the name, training name, date passed, “valid until”, organisation and logo, trainer, and a verification code with a QR code. The code is the same as on the result report.
- Anyone who knows the verification code can check on the verification page whether the certificate is genuine and still valid. After the contract with the operator ends, certificates can still be verified for 5 years. Certificates with a longer validity can no longer be verified after that.
- The test taker downloads it from the result page or from **My results**. You download it in the result details with **Certificate (PDF)**.
- Your organisation issues the certificate. The application states on it that it is not issued by a public authority or an accredited body. It also states that the certificate does not by itself establish a professional competence that the law ties to an exam before an authority.
- An expired certificate can still be downloaded, but the PDF states that it has expired.

After personal data is anonymised, or after a paper exam is corrected, the certificate can no longer be downloaded. The result details give the reason.

## Trainee confirmation

On the summary before submitting, the test taker sees the training name, trainer and syllabus. They tick the text “I confirm that I have been briefed on the content of the training “…” and that I understand it.” Without ticking it, they cannot submit the test themselves. Test takers learn about the confirmation on the test start page.

Fill in the **Training syllabus** field. Without a syllabus, the confirmation refers only to the training name. The record will not show what the employee was briefed on.

- The confirmation is saved in the exam record with the time and the test taker’s name. Like answers, it cannot be changed after submission.
- If time runs out or you end the attempt, the test is submitted without confirmation. The result details show **Not confirmed** and the reason.
- Confirmation is not collected for paper entry.

## Training record

1. Open **Results** and choose a test in the filter, and a period if needed (**From**, **To**). Or open the results of a single test assignment.
2. Select **Training record (PDF)** or **Training record (XLSX)**.

The record contains the organisation, the training name and syllabus, the trainer, the period and a list of the people trained. For each person, it shows name, staff number, email, confirmation time, test result, certificate expiry and verification code. It includes only people whose results you are allowed to see (instructors their own assignments, branch administrators their branch).

- Attempts from a link without a name are not in the record, because they do not prove that a named person was briefed. The record states how many there were.
- At the end, the PDF has optional fields for the signatures of the trainer and the responsible person, if you also want to sign the record on paper.

## Things to keep in mind

Your organisation is responsible for the content and scope of the training. The training must really take place. The test and confirmation do not replace it, they only provide evidence of it. The Czech Labour Code (Section 103) requires employers to set the content and frequency of training and the way knowledge is checked, and to keep training records.

According to a statement by the State Labour Inspection Office (ref. 2932/1.30/22-2 of 2 May 2022), the employer chooses the form of the record. Instead of signatures on an attendance sheet, a unique electronic record can be used. It must prove that each employee was briefed by name. Whether the record is enough in a particular case is for the inspection to decide. So the application does not claim that electronic confirmation always replaces a signature.

How strong the confirmation is as evidence depends on how you make sure the right employee took the test:

- ideally, by assigning the test to a learner with their own account and password,
- or with a personal access code sent to the employee’s work email.

A code passed to someone else, a shared password or a link without a name weakens the evidence. The training record also lists people who did not pass the test or did not confirm the briefing. You decide who counts as trained according to your own rules.
