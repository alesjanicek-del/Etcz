Record the dates of real exams. The operator does not install new versions of the application at those times.

## New exam date

1. Open **Tests and exams → Exam calendar**.
2. Select **Add date**.
3. Fill in **Name**, **Start** and **End** (Prague time) and, if you like, **Test assignment (optional)**.
4. Select **Save**.

## Why record exam dates

During an exam, and for 2 hours before and after it, the operator does not install new versions of the application or schedule maintenance. So record the dates of all real exams.

## Editing and deleting

You can edit or delete an exam date in the calendar overview.

*For roles: Instructor, Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
