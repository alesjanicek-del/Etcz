## Uncertain points

- **Uncrawled articles.** Three categories link to articles that are not in the crawl: “Hlášení vady a požadavku na podporu” and “Lhůty a kontakty podpory” (objednatel), “Koš a obnovení” (autor) and “Osobní údaje, anonymizace a export dat” (admin-organizace). Their slugs are unknown, so they are plain text with an HTML `TODO link` comment. In the EN text, their titles are proposals: “Reporting a fault and support requests”, “Support response times and contacts”, “Deleted items and restoring”, “Personal data, anonymisation and data export”.
- **Help home page (`index.md`).** The home page changes by role. This version is the Test taker one from `zkouseny__napoveda.txt`. The search box is left out because the app provides it. Links to other roles’ categories point to `<category>/index.md`.
- **Category pages.** Their “task” links (for example, “Jak se přihlásit”) point to anchors inside articles in the app. Here they link to the article only, because the anchor IDs change once headings are translated.
- **Left out on purpose.** The breadcrumb, “Na této stránce” (generated table of contents), previous/next links and the print tip are left out. They are part of the article template, not the content.
- **“Pro role:” line.** Kept as the last line in italics (“For roles: …”).
- **Exam monitoring article.** The CS text calls the automatic submission decision a decision “by a program, not a person” (GDPR Art. 22 wording). The EN “decided by software, not a person” should be checked by legal.
- **Long sentences.** A few EN sentences are 26–34 words. They are mostly lists inside one sentence or table cells. Shorten them if the app’s check is strict.
