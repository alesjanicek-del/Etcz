## Uncertain points

- **Article counts.** I was asked for 15 lektor and 8 autor articles. The crawl and the category pages have only 14 lektor and 7 autor articles. Two autor articles listed on the category page are missing from the crawl: “Import a export otázek (XLSX)” and “Koš a obnovení”. They appear in both index.md files as plain headings with an HTML comment. I did not create those articles.
- **Link targets not in the crawl.** Two link targets are plain text, with no link: “Osobní údaje, anonymizace a export dat” (zkouska-zoz, vysledky-a-protokol) and “Koš” (hledani-otazek). objednatel/zalohy-a-obnova-dat uses the guessed slugs `../autor/kos-a-obnoveni.md` and `../admin-organizace/osobni-udaje.md`. If those slugs are confirmed, add the same links here.
- **Czech law references** (Labour Code Sections 103 and 316(3), SÚIP ref. 2932/1.30/22-2) are translated descriptively. They are not official translations of the law.
- **Sample data** such as “Jan Novák (1985)” and `Jan Novák; ; 50` stays in Czech. The search example `zareni -alfa` became `radiation -alpha`.
- **Screen reader example**: “caesium one hundred and thirty-seven”. This is British spelling. A Czech screen reader would still need Czech text.
- The text of the training acknowledgement checkbox (“I confirm that I have been briefed on…”) and the warning “You left the test window. We have recorded this.” are UI strings. Check them against the EN catalogue.
