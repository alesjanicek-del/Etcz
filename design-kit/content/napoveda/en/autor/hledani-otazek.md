Search the question text, answers and note. Filter by date, author, status and tags.

**Question banks → Search questions** searches all the banks you have access to. The same filter is also in the bank details.

## Filter

- **Full text (question, answers, note)** also works without Czech diacritics. Put a phrase in quotation marks. Exclude a word with a minus sign, for example `radiation -alpha`.
- **Note contains** searches only the **Internal note** field.
- **Author**, **Created from** and **Created to**, **Status** and **Question banks** narrow the results.
- **Tags**, with the option **at least one of the selected** or **all selected**.

## Why can’t I find a question?

- You have no permission for the bank it is in. Ask the bank owner for permission.
- The question is in Deleted items (see Deleted items).
- The filter is too narrow. Try fewer conditions.

*For roles: Question author, Instructor, Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
