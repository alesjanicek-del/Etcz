Creating a question bank, its details, and granting permissions to colleagues, a branch or the whole organisation.

Questions are stored in question banks. A bank is a named topic area, for example “Radiation protection”. Tender documents may also call it a topic or a question pool. Permissions are granted for each bank.

## Question bank list

The **Questions → Question banks** menu shows the banks you have access to. For each bank, you see the branch, the owner, **Your permission**, the number of questions and the number of questions **Pending approval**.

## New question bank

1. Select **New question bank**.
2. Fill in **Question bank name** and, if you like, **Description**.
3. Select **Create bank**.

The bank is created in your active branch, and you become its owner.

Can’t see the button? Your organisation may not allow instructors to create banks. Ask your administrator.

## Bank details

The bank details have the buttons **New question**, **Import XLSX**, **Export XLSX**, **Edit**, **Permissions** and **Move to Deleted items**. Below them are a filter and a table of questions.

Change the order of questions with the ↑ ↓ arrows. The arrows are shown only when no filter is active.

## Bank permissions

Only the bank owner can grant permissions.

1. In the bank details, select **Permissions**.
2. Under **Add permission**, choose who gets the permission: **User**, **Branch** (all its members) or **Whole organisation**.
3. In **Permission level**, choose **Read**, **Edit** or **Owner**. What each level allows is described in [Roles and permissions](../zaciname/role-a-opravneni.md).
4. Select **Add permission**.

**Transfer ownership** hands the bank over to another user. The previous owner keeps edit permission.

## What if a colleague cannot see the bank

Your colleague needs permission for the bank: directly, through a branch where they have a role, or for the whole organisation. The active branch makes no difference. Grant them permission as described above.

*For roles: Question author, Instructor, Branch administrator, Organisation administrator*
