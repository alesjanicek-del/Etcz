Question text, answers, correct answers, shuffling, points, answer feedback, tags and internal note.

## Where to create a question

- In the bank details, select **New question**.
- Or select **New question** on the **Overview** page. The application first asks which bank to add the question to.

## Question

- **Question type**: **Single correct answer** or **Multiple correct answers**. A question with multiple correct answers counts as correct only if the test taker marks all the correct answers and no incorrect one. Partial points can be allowed in the grading method.
- Write the **Question text** in the editor with its toolbar (see [Editor, images and accessibility](editor-a-pristupnost.md)).

## Answers

- A question must have at least 4 and at most 10 answers.
- Add another answer with **+ Add answer**. Remove an extra one with **Remove**.
- Mark correct answers by ticking **Correct**.
- **Pin in place**: the answer keeps its position when answers are shuffled. This is useful for “None of the above”.
- **Shuffle answers**: each test taker sees the answers in a different order, except pinned ones.

## Scoring and answer feedback

- **Points**: 1 to 100.
- **Feedback for a correct answer** and **Feedback for an incorrect answer** are optional. Test takers see them according to the test settings: after evaluation, or straight after they answer. They are never shown in an official test.

## Tags and note

- **Tags**: tick tags from the lists you are allowed to use (see [Tags](stitky.md)).
- **Internal note**, for example “Source: Decree No. 422/2016 Coll.”. Test takers never see it, and it is not included in printed question papers.
- Fill in **Change summary** when you edit a question. It is shown in the version history.

## Saving

Select **Save question**.

If your organisation has approval turned on, you see **Save as draft** and **Submit for approval** instead (see [Question approval](schvalovani-otazek.md)).

If the question breaks the rules, a summary of errors appears at the top, with links to the fields concerned. For example:

- “The question must have at least 4 answers.”
- “Mark at least one correct answer.”
- “Add alternative text for the image in the question.” (fill in the description, or mark the image as **Decorative**; see [Editor, images and accessibility](editor-a-pristupnost.md))

## Unsaved content after you are signed out

The editor keeps saving your unsaved question content in this browser tab. If the application signs you out before you save the question (for example, after inactivity), you see **Unsaved draft content** at the top when you sign in again and open the editor:

- **Restore unsaved content** puts what you had written back into the form. Then save the question as usual.
- **Discard unsaved content** deletes it, and the editor keeps the last saved version.

Unsaved content is stored only in this browser tab and disappears when you close it. You cannot see it on another computer or in another tab.

## Editing a question

If the bank is used in tests, the editor warns you before you edit. Every change to the content creates a new version of the question:

- new test versions get the new question version,
- attempts in progress and printed test versions keep the original version,
- copies of the question in other banks do not change.

## Question details

The question details have the buttons **Edit**, **Preview as test taker** (without correct answers and the note) and **History**. Approvers also see **Approve**.

Under **More actions** are **Copy to bank**, **Move to bank** and **Move to Deleted items**. When you move a question, tags from the original bank’s lists are removed.

*For roles: Question author, Instructor, Organisation administrator*
