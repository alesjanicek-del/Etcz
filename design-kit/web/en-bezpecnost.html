Measures in the app

Organisations and branches kept apart

Every database query is automatically limited to your organisation. Without an organisation, the app returns no data.

Branches see only their own data, and another branch’s record behaves as if it did not exist. Automated tests check this separation on every change.

Two-step verification

Sign-in with a password and a code from an app on your phone. It is required for organisation and branch administrators, question authors and anyone who can edit question banks or tests. You can require it for other instructors too.

Backup codes cover a lost phone. Sign-in by email link is off by default. If you turn it on, it replaces only the password, not the second step.

Change log

Changes to questions, tests, users, roles and settings are written to a log that cannot be edited later. The organisation administrator sees it in the app. Entries are kept for 60 months, then deleted.

Evidential results

Submitted attempts are written to a chained record; any later change shows up on verification. Each result report carries a verification code, and anyone can verify it online.

Support only with your consent

A support engineer enters your organisation only when an administrator allows it (permanently or for a time window). Every access has a stated reason, is written to the change log, and administrators get an email about it.

Your data, your exit

Export question banks to XLSX and results to spreadsheets. Download an export of all your organisation’s data at any time, even before the contract ends. If you ask before the contract ends, we will hand it over after it ends too.

Protecting test takers’ personal data

The organisation sets how long results are kept. After that, test takers’ personal data is irreversibly anonymised, including the stored PDF report with their name. The report stays verifiable without the name.

Protect a record you must keep longer from anonymisation. After a restore from backup, earlier anonymisations are repeated. Test takers find information about data processing right in the app. We store access codes only in irreversible form, and two-step verification data encrypted.

Platform administration

The operator’s administration is available only from allowed IP addresses and with two-step verification. Deleting an organisation or restoring data from backup must be proposed by one operator employee and approved by another.

The app uses the database under an account that cannot change the change log or submitted results.

Website protection

Encrypted connections, a strict Content Security Policy, protection against forged form submissions, and limits on sign-in and code entry attempts.

Operations and hosting

Processor and sub-processors

For organisations that use exam-test, the operator ABUCO ICT s.r.o. is the processor of test takers’ and users’ personal data under Article 28 GDPR. The terms are set out in the data processing agreement.

The list of sub-processors (hosting, email delivery) is an annex to the data processing agreement. We inform organisations of any change at least 30 days in advance. The timestamping service receives only a cryptographic hash, not personal data.
