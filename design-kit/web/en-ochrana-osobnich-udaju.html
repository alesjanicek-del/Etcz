Summary

At a glance

  • We process the data of test takers and users only on the instructions of the organisation that uses the service. That organisation is the controller and handles your requests.
  • As a controller, we mainly process website enquiries, registrations, invoicing and communication with support. We keep enquiries for 12 months.
  • The data are on servers in Germany (EU). We do not transfer them outside the European Economic Area.
  • We use only strictly necessary cookies, so we do not need your consent. We have no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Send your requests to podpora@exam-test.cz. You can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection.

The summary is for orientation only and does not replace the text of the document. The full text below is binding.

Information under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) on how the operator of the exam-test service processes the personal data of website visitors, prospective customers and customer contact persons.

Who is the controller

The controller of the personal data described in this policy is ABUCO ICT s.r.o., with its registered office at Třebohostická 987/5, 100 00 Praha 10, Company ID No. (IČO) 29153905, registered in the Commercial Register maintained by the Municipal Court in Prague, Section C, Insert 204821 (“we”). Contact for personal data protection matters: podpora@exam-test.cz. We have not appointed a data protection officer; we handle all questions and requests concerning personal data at this address.

Data of test takers and users of organisations

We do not process the data of persons who take exams in the application and of users of organisations (instructors, authors, administrators) as a controller, but as a processor on the instructions of the organisation that uses the service. The controller is that organisation; the terms are set out in the data processing agreement. Test takers find the information on processing in the application at the organisation’s address (the “Information on personal data processing” link on the test start page). Requests for access to, rectification or erasure of these data are handled by the organisation; if you send them to us, we will forward them to it.

What data we process as a controller, why and for how long

  • Website enquiries (the “Arrange a demo” form and contact): name, organisation, email, telephone, message text. Purpose: replying to the enquiry and negotiating a contract. Legal basis: taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) or, if you act on behalf of an organisation, our legitimate interest in replying to the enquiry (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep them for 12 months from the end of the month in which the enquiry arrived; if a contract is concluded, the rules for registration and order data apply from then on. To protect the form against misuse, we store the sender’s IP address only in an irreversibly truncated form and delete it together with the enquiry.
  • Registration, orders and invoicing: organisation name, Company ID No. (IČO), VAT number (DIČ), billing address, name and email of the contact person and of the administrator. Purpose: concluding and performing the contract, invoicing, debt recovery. Legal basis: performance of the contract with the customer and our legitimate interest in communicating with its contact persons (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR); for accounting and tax documents, compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). We keep them for the term of the contract and then for the limitation period for claims (usually 3 years); accounting and tax documents for the period laid down by accounting and tax regulations (up to 10 years).
  • Communication and technical support: the name, email, telephone and request text you send us. Purpose: handling the request and evaluating the level of service. Legal basis: performance of the contract and legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We anonymise the contact details and text of resolved requests in the application 24 months after resolution.
  • We send operational emails (invitations, sign-in links, end of the trial period, tips for getting started, invoices, limit warnings and security alerts) to users and administrators of organisations as part of performing the contract. We do not send commercial communications without consent; an exception may be offers of similar services to existing customers under Section 7(3) of Act No. 480/2004 Coll., which can be refused at any time.
  • Security and operational logs: IP address, time and requested address in the server’s technical logs and in the limit on sign-in attempts; a record of actions by the operator’s staff (platform change log: who, what, when and why). Purpose: securing the service, detecting and resolving incidents, preventing misuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the security of the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The server’s technical logs are continuously overwritten (usually within days to weeks), the limit on sign-in attempts lasts minutes to hours; we keep the platform change log for as long as necessary to demonstrate the proper provision of the service.
  • Public demo (the public demo link on the website): the demo requires no registration, name or email, and no emails are sent from it. Everything you create or fill in in the demo is a fictitious sample – do not enter personal or other real data in it. The entire content of the demo is deleted every night at 3:00. We use the IP address briefly to protect against misuse: from one address, we limit the number of demo starts (at most 5 per hour), code entries and help searches. These records expire within two hours at the latest. If you complete a test in the demo as a test taker, the IP address and browser type are stored in the technical log of the attempt, just as in live operation; they are deleted with the entire demo content at night at 3:00. We measure the use of the demo only as aggregate daily counts (for example, how many times it was started in which role) without personal data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in demonstrating the service and protecting it against misuse (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Vulnerability reports (email from the security page): the data you send us. Purpose: fixing the error. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We obtain the data directly from you, or from the organisation on whose behalf you act (e.g. when an administrator invites you as a user). We may verify the Company ID No. and name of the organisation in public registers. Providing data at registration and when ordering is a contractual requirement; without them, the service cannot be set up or invoiced. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

Cookies

We use only cookies and browser storage that are strictly necessary for the service to work as you request it. We therefore do not need your consent to use them. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, measurement scripts or third-party content (fonts, maps, videos).

  • Session (__Host-ETSESS): sign-in, two-step verification, access to an exam entered with a code, and form protection. Valid until the browser is closed. The sign-in ends on signing out, after inactivity (in administration after 30 minutes, for students after 60 minutes, in the operator console after 15 minutes) and no later than 12 hours after sign-in (operator console 4 hours). The server deletes other session data, for example access to an exam entered with a code, after 12 hours without activity at the latest.
  • Form protection (__Host-csrf-token_…): a one-time random value against cross-site request forgery. Valid until the browser is closed.
  • Attempt in progress (__Host-et_a_…): links a test in progress to your browser so that no one can complete it from another device. Valid for 1 day.
  • Supervised in-room exam (__Host-et_l_…): recognises your device in the waiting room of a supervised in-room exam. Valid until the browser is closed.
  • Public demo (demo_visit): only at the demo address; recognises that you are returning and takes you back to your sample branch. Contains a random visit number and the chosen role. Valid until the next nightly deletion of the demo (3:00).
  • Browser session storage (sessionStorage): during a test, temporarily holds answers that have not yet been saved to the server because of a connection outage, and notices for the test taker (exam-queue-…, exam-notice-…, exam-announced-…). For a test with exam monitoring and a full-screen offer, it also remembers that you were taking the test in full screen so that full screen can be restored on the next question (exam-fs-…, a yes/no flag only). Unsaved answers are sent as an ordinary answer save once the connection is restored; the other values remain only in your browser. In the question editor, the draft content of a question is saved here continuously so that you can restore it after being signed out for inactivity (exam-test:question-draft:…, the key contains identifiers of the organisation and your account); the draft is deleted on signing out, after 12 hours at the latest. Everything in this storage is deleted when the browser tab is closed.
  • Browser local storage (localStorage): the time of your last activity in administration, so that the warning before sign-out applies equally in all open tabs (exam-test:session-activity), and the choice to pause the automatic refresh of the live monitor (exam-test:frame-refresh-paused:…, a yes/no flag only). It contains no personal data and remains in the browser even after it is closed until you delete it; the time value is overwritten on your next activity.

Your rights

You have the right of access to your data, to rectification and erasure, to restriction of processing and to data portability (for data processed on the basis of a contract). You may object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest; an objection to commercial communications always takes effect. We will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest. Write to us at podpora@exam-test.cz.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.
