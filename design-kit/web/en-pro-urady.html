Typical use

One app for the whole exam

Professional competence exams

An official test with required questions and random picks from question banks. Exclude outdated questions with a tag instead of deleting them.

Supervised in-room exam

Candidates join with a code or QR code. You admit them to the test and follow the live monitor. Everyone gets a different test version.

Print on paper

Where there is no computer, print test versions with answer sheets. Enter the paper results and keep them in the same records.

What a public body needs

Law, GDPR, records and procurement

Accessibility under Act No. 99/2019 Coll.

The interface is designed to WCAG 2.1 AA (EN 301 549). The app is partially compliant so far. We list known gaps in the accessibility statement, as required by Decree No. 523/2020 Coll.

You are the obliged body. Take over the draft statement with its technical background, add your contact details and publish it.

Accessibility statement

GDPR and the data processing agreement

The public body is the controller and the operator is the processor under Article 28 GDPR. Candidates find information about data processing right in the app. You set how long results are kept; after that, the data is anonymised.

Data processing agreement (template)

Evidential value of results

A result report with a verification code, a chained record of all submitted attempts and a timeline for every attempt. Any later change to a result can be detected.

Whether the report is sufficient evidence in administrative proceedings is for your body to decide, under the rules that apply to the exam.

Official records and their protection

Exclude selected attempts from automatic anonymisation if you must keep them longer, for example for the duration of proceedings. Official test mode turns off public links, and you release results to candidates manually.

The app does not replace a records management system. Export reports and results and file them according to your filing and retention plan.

Frequently asked questions from public bodies

Does the app comply with the accessibility act?

Partially, so far. The interface is designed to WCAG 2.1 level AA (EN 301 549). We list known gaps in the accessibility statement under Decree No. 523/2020 Coll. and fix them on an ongoing basis. Your body is the obliged body under Act No. 99/2019 Coll. Take over the statement as a draft, add your contact details and publish it.

How is personal data protected?

The public body is the controller and the operator is the processor under Article 28 GDPR. We sign a data processing agreement. You set how long results are kept; after that, candidates’ data is anonymised. Official records can be excluded from anonymisation (record protection).

Can an exam result be used as evidence in administrative proceedings?

The app creates a result report with a verification code for every submitted attempt. Each record is chained to the previous ones, so any later change can be detected. Anyone can verify a report online. Whether the report is sufficient evidence is for your body to decide, under the rules that apply to the exam.

Is the service in the cloud computing catalogue?

Not yet. Under Act No. 365/2000 Coll., public administration bodies may use cloud computing listed in the catalogue kept by the Digital and Information Agency, with exceptions set by law. Before buying, assess whether this obligation applies to your purpose. We are happy to provide documents on our security measures.

Will the app replace our records management system?

No. The app is a tool for preparing and running exams. Export reports and results (PDF and XLSX) and file them according to your filing and retention plan. Set the result retention period in the app to match it. Whether the exam meets the requirements of the rules that apply to it is for your body to decide.

How do we buy the service?

Most often as a small-scale public contract. We send a price quote and documents for your call. We agree each contract individually, including the support level and deadlines for fixing defects. We invoice according to the contract.

Can we have the app on our own domain with our own logo?

Yes. Your organisation can have its own address (such as zkousky.vas-urad.cz) and its own logo. Candidates then see no advertising and no links to the operator, apart from the required details.
