Pricing questions

What is an attempt?

An attempt is one person completing a test once. It is counted when a test version is created: when an online test starts, when someone is admitted to a supervised in-room exam, and for every printed sheet in a print batch. Test previews and trial runs are not counted.

What happens when we run out of attempts?

At 80% and 100% of the limit, we notify the organisation’s administrators. New attempts can be started up to 110% of the limit. After that, we block them until you move to a higher plan. During the free trial, you cannot go over the limit. We never interrupt an exam in progress.

How does invoicing work?

Order a plan in the app. We send a proforma invoice payable within 14 days, and a tax invoice after payment. We activate the plan for 12 months once payment arrives; a renewal paid in advance follows on from the current period. The subscription never renews or bills automatically. We remind you 30 and 7 days before it ends. Prices exclude VAT.

Can we buy the service as a small-scale public contract?

Yes. For public bodies and state-funded organisations, we prepare a price quote and documents for your call: technical specification, security measures overview and the data processing agreement template. We invoice according to the contract, with no advance payment.

What if we want to leave?

Your data is not locked in. Export question banks to XLSX and results to spreadsheets at any time. Download an export of all your organisation’s data in the app until the contract ends. If you ask before the contract ends, we will hand it over afterwards too. We then delete the data within 60 days of the contract ending.

How much do test templates and services cost?

Templates are free for now. If we start charging, we will announce it in advance, and templates you take over before then stay free of charge. We price services by the amount of work (number of questions, users and branches, format of materials). We send a free quote within 2 working days.

How does the free trial work?

The free trial lasts 30 days and includes 100 attempts, 1 branch and 3 users with the administrator, instructor or author role (including you; students are not counted). You don’t need a payment card. When it ends, your data is kept and the account is read-only until you order a plan.
