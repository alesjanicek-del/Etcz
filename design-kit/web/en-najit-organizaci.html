Sign in to your organisation

Every organisation has its own address in exam-test, for example organisation-name.exam-test.cz. That’s where you sign in.

Taking a test or exam?

You don’t need to sign in. Open the link or address from your instructor or the exam organiser and enter your access code. Your code won’t work here – it belongs to your organisation’s address.

I know my organisation’s address
