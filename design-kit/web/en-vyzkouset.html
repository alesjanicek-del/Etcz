Before you start the demo

  • Data in the demo is made up and is deleted every night at 3:00, including your changes.
  • Please don’t enter personal or real data, such as names, emails or questions from your exams.
More information

After deletion, the demo is set up again, so the next day you start with a new demo branch. The demo sends no emails. You’ll find invitations, codes and results in the app, in the Demo mailbox.
