Frequently asked questions from companies

Is the app suitable for health and safety training and periodic re-testing?

Yes. Prepare a question bank, build the test once and assign it to employees or send them a link. See all employees’ results in one place and export them to a spreadsheet. Every attempt produces a report for your records.

Do employees need an account?

No. Deliver the test with personal access codes, a public link or as a supervised in-room exam via QR code. An account is useful where you want one person’s result history kept together.

We have several sites. Will each see only its own data?

Yes. An organisation is divided into branches. Branch instructors and administrators see only their branch’s data; the organisation administrator sees everything. Question banks can be shared across branches with read or edit permissions.

Can we convert our current questions?

Yes. Import questions from an XLSX sheet using the template you download in the app. For larger banks or other formats, we can help with the conversion – see services to order.
