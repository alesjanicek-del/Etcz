Frequently asked questions

Does it prevent cheating?

Not completely, and we don’t claim it does. The app does not lock the computer. But everyone gets a different version, and the instructor sees who left the window, copied text or lost the connection. For high-stakes exams we recommend a supervised exam in the room.

Can people with disabilities take the test?

Yes. The test taker interface is designed to WCAG 2.1 AA and works with a keyboard. Instructors can give individual people extra time. Known gaps are listed in the accessibility statement (in Czech).

How much does it cost?

Companies and schools pay an annual plan from CZK 9,900 excl. VAT, based on attempts, branches and users. Public bodies receive an individual quote. Pricing (in Czech)

Our tests are in Word or on paper. How do we convert them?

Import questions from an XLSX sheet. We convert Word, PDF, Moodle or paper tests for you – free quote within 2 working days.

Can we test on paper?

Yes. Print any number of versions with answer sheets and keys. The instructor enters the paper answers and they are scored just like online.

Can we leave at any time?

Yes. Export questions and results any time. After the contract ends, we delete the data within 60 days.
