How it will work

  1. 01

    Choose a template

    By topic and target group. For each template we state the date of the regulations it reflects.

  2. 02

    Add it to your account

    The organisation administrator confirms it, including the terms and the notice that the template has not been reviewed by experts. The template becomes your question bank.

    Until the catalogue is in the app, we send it on request as an import file.

  3. 03

    Edit and approve

    Add your own policies and risks to the questions and delete what you don’t need. Your subject-matter expert approves the changes.

  4. 04

    Start testing

    Online, in the room or on paper. Every test taker gets a different test version and a report with their result.

Frequently asked questions about templates

What exactly is a catalogue template?

A ready-made question bank on one topic, such as health and safety or GDPR, with a suggested test. Add the template to your account and edit, add or delete questions to suit your regulations and risks. Then use it as your own question bank.

Who is responsible for the questions being correct?

The organisation that uses the template. Templates have not been reviewed by experts. They are starting points we prepare from publicly available regulations as at the date stated in the template. The operator is not responsible for their accuracy, completeness or compliance with regulations. Before use, a qualified person in your organisation must check and adapt the template.

Does a test replace statutory training or an exam?

No. A test checks knowledge. It does not replace training the employer must provide, or an exam or assessment that the law reserves for an authorised person, committee or authority. Passing a test does not in itself grant any authorisation or professional competence.

How much does a template cost and how do we get it?

Templates are free for now. If we start charging, we will announce it in advance, and templates you take over before then stay free of charge. The in-app catalogue is in preparation. Until then, we send templates on request as a question bank file that you import into your account.

Can we edit a template and use it without limits?

Yes, within your organisation. There is no limit on test takers or tests from one template. Attempts count towards your plan, as with any other test.

Which services can we order and what do they cost?

Help onboarding your organisation, and question import or conversion – see Services to order. We price by the amount of work (number of questions, users and branches, format of materials). We send a free quote within 2 working days. Order a service with the form; send files only after the quote.
