Guided demo

We’ll show you exam-test with your own exam

Leave us your contact details. We’ll get back to you within 2 working days to agree a date. Then we’ll go through the app with you online in about 30 minutes, using your type of exam.

  • We show the whole path from question to report: building a test, a supervised in-room exam, print and result verification.
  • We discuss your requirements: number of test takers, branches, accessibility, procurement.
