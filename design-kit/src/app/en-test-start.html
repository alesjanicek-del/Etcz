Organiser’s instructions

Exam instructions

The test has 12 questions and a time limit of 15 minutes. The pass mark is 80%. You have 3 attempts.

This is a sample exam in the public demo. The data is not real and is deleted every night.

{{I:oko}}Monitoring is on

We record when you leave the window, leave full screen, copy or paste. No camera or microphone is used.

What exactly is recorded
  • Leaving the test window or tab – switching elsewhere, and on a phone also turning off the screen.
  • Leaving full screen. If your browser or assistive technology does not support it, take the test as normal.
  • Copying text and pasting from the clipboard.

If you leave the window, you will see a notice when you come back. Your instructor sees the record with your result and in the result report. It is for information only – a person decides what happens next.

Do you use assistive technology that takes over the window, such as dictation? Tell your instructor before you start. You do not have to give a diagnosis.

How we process data during monitoring

More about this test

We record how the test goes: when you start, which questions you view, changes to answers, leaving the window and submitting.

Do you need more time because of a disability or health condition? Ask your instructor before you start. They can also give you extra time during the test.
