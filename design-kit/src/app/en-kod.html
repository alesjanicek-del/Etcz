No account needed

Enter a test

You received the code from the organiser, on paper or by email. It has 10 characters. Dashes, spaces and capital letters do not matter.

You can also enter the code after submitting. If the test allows another attempt, you will start it. Otherwise you will see your result once the organiser releases it.

Other ways into a test

Join a supervised in-room exam

Are you taking the exam in a room? You will find the code on the projector screen.

Enter a handover code

Did you start the test on another device, for example your phone battery ran out? Your instructor will give you the code.

Sign in to your learner account

Have an account? You will find your assigned tests in My tests.
