2.1 The Service is a web application for creating questions, building tests, testing online, in the room and on paper, evaluation and issuing result reports. The attempt limit applies to one prepaid period of 12 months and, during the trial period, to the whole trial period.

2.3 An attempt means one completion of a test by one person; it is counted when a test version is generated (start online, admission to a supervised in-room exam, each printed sheet). Previews, trial runs and attempts on the sample data inserted at the start of the trial period are not counted.

2.4 The attempt limit of a paid plan is soft: the Provider notifies the Customer’s administrators at 80% and 100% of the limit, blocks the start of new attempts only above 110% of the limit and never interrupts an attempt in progress. During the trial period the limit is hard: once it is used up, no new attempts can be started. Attempts above the limit up to 110% are not charged separately.
