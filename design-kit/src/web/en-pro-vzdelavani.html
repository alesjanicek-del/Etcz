Frequently asked questions from schools and instructors

How do you stop students copying?

Every student gets their own test version: a different selection of questions from the bank, and a different order of questions and answers. In a supervised in-room exam, you admit students to the test yourself and see their status on the live monitor. The app does not lock the computer, so it does not replace supervision in the room.

Can we print the test?

Yes. A print batch creates any number of different test versions, answer sheets and separate answer keys for marking. Enter the paper results in the app and keep them together with the online results.

Can the app handle chemical and mathematical notation?

The question editor supports subscript and superscript, tables and images. You can add screen reader text to formulas, so students with visual impairments can take the test too.

How quickly will we see which questions are badly worded?

Statistics show, for every question and its versions, how often it was used in a test and how often students answered it correctly. When you correct a question, a new version is created. Past attempts stay scored against the original wording.
