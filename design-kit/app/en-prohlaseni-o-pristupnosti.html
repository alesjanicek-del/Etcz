Accessibility statement

Ukázková organizace (demo) · last reviewed 1 Nov 2026

Unofficial translation.

The Czech version is legally binding. Prohlášení o přístupnosti (Czech version)

At a glance

  • The application is partially compliant with Act No. 99/2019 Coll. (WCAG 2.1 at level AA).
  • Known issues: images and notation from authors without a description, and large PDF print batches without tags. A manual screen reader test is still missing.
  • Need more time for a test? Ask the instructor or the exam organiser in advance.
  • You get a warning before sign-out for inactivity. Extend your sign-in with the button or the Esc key.
  • Report an issue to podpora@exam-test.cz. We will reply within 30 days.

The summary is for orientation only. The full text below is binding.

Ukázková organizace (demo), as an obliged body under Section 2 of Act No. 99/2019 Coll., undertakes to make the exam-test web application (tests and exams of the organisation Ukázková organizace (demo)) accessible in accordance with Act No. 99/2019 Coll., on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications and amending Act No. 365/2000 Coll., on public administration information systems and amending certain other acts, as amended. The accessibility requirements are laid down by the harmonised standard ČSN EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (corresponding to WCAG 2.1 at level AA).

This accessibility statement applies to the exam-test application at the address at which Ukázková organizace (demo) runs it: the interface for test takers (entry with a code, supervised in-room exam, completing a test, submission, result), public verification of result reports and administration (instructors, question authors, administrators).

Compliance status

This web application is partially compliant with Act No. 99/2019 Coll. due to the non-compliances listed below.

Non-accessible content

a) Non-compliance with Act No. 99/2019 Coll.

Based on a self-assessment (1 Nov 2026: automated check with the axe tool, manual check with a keyboard and the accessibility tree, display at widths from 320 to 1440 px and analysis of the structure of PDF files), the following issues are known; we are gradually fixing them:

  • Content added by authors: images in questions without a sufficiently descriptive alternative text, and mathematical or chemical notation (subscripts) without “text for screen readers” (criteria 1.1.1 and 1.3.1). The organisation that prepared the test is responsible for this content.
  • Print batch with more than 50 test versions: the application assembles it from parts and the merged PDF file (test papers and, where applicable, answer keys) has no structure tags; a screen reader may not recognise headings, lists and tables in it (criteria 1.3.1 and 1.3.2). Smaller batches are tagged like other PDF files (see below). On request, we will provide the content of the document in an accessible form.
  • The interface has not yet been manually tested with a screen reader or in Windows high contrast mode (including the announcement of a new page after navigation in administration and the warning before sign-out). Testing is planned; we will add any issues found to this list.

Fixed in the version of 27 Oct 2026: the question editor lets you enter an image description, edit the description and enter text for screen readers in an accessible dialog (keyboard operation, Esc cancels, focus returns to the button, errors are shown at the field); an image that is purely decorative can be marked as decorative – a screen reader skips it and in print and PDF it has an empty alternative text. A screen reader reliably announces changes to the test section summary and to the status of a print batch.

Fixed in the version of 28 Oct 2026:

  • links in text are underlined (they used to differ by colour only, criterion 1.4.1),
  • the projector screen of a supervised in-room exam refreshes without automatically reloading the whole page (criterion 2.2.1),
  • error messages for sign-in, two-step verification and forms were partly in English; they are now in Czech and say what to do (criteria 3.1.2 and 3.3.1),
  • a field with an error is marked as invalid for screen readers and the error message is associated with it; a submitted form with errors has a summary at the top with links to the fields and takes focus (criteria 1.3.1 and 3.3.1),
  • groups of radio buttons are grouped with a label and “required” is read only once (criteria 1.3.1 and 3.3.2),
  • a time extension by the instructor is shown to the test taker and announced to screen readers; the test taker is told when the instructor has ended their test (criterion 4.1.3),
  • the field for a two-step verification backup code opens a keyboard with letters on a phone (criterion 3.3.2),
  • tables that do not fit on a narrow screen can be scrolled with a keyboard (criterion 2.1.1),
  • sign-out works on all pages and a confirmation is shown after signing out.

Fixed in the version of 29 Oct 2026:

  • pages reflow at a width of 320 px (400% zoom) without horizontal scrolling, including the organisation settings, question import, the test wizard and the test header (criterion 1.4.10),
  • all links in the page content are underlined, including outside paragraphs and lists, e.g. in cards and messages (criterion 1.4.1),
  • after a monitoring warning is closed, focus returns to where it was, not to the top of the page; the Esc key also closes the warning (criterion 2.4.3),
  • answer fields in the question editor and sign-in fields have an error message associated with the field or group of fields (criteria 1.3.1 and 3.3.1),
  • the Esc key closes the application’s mobile menu and focus returns to the menu button.

Fixed in the version of 31 Oct 2026:

  • 2 minutes before sign-out for inactivity (administration after 30 minutes, students after 60 minutes, operator console after 15 minutes), a warning is shown with an “Extend sign-in” button that extends the sign-in; a question being written in the editor is saved continuously in this browser tab and can be restored after signing in again, which returns you to the original page (criterion 2.2.1),
  • the automatic refresh of the instructor’s live monitor can be paused and switched on again, and a screen reader announces the change of state; the projector screen of a supervised in-room exam continues to refresh by itself, as that is its purpose (criterion 2.2.2),
  • after navigating to another administration page, focus moves to the page heading and a screen reader announces its title (criteria 2.4.3 and 4.1.3),
  • PDF files generated by the application (exam result reports, certificates, training records, instructions for test takers, manuals, printed test versions, answer sheets and answer keys, accounting documents) are tagged PDFs with the document language; tables have row and column header cells, and a table that only repeats the header on the pages of the test paper is not announced to screen readers as a table. Verified by automated structure analysis; a check with the PAC tool is planned (criterion 1.3.1),
  • at a width of 320 px, tag editing, question statistics, test permissions, user details and the public demo overview in the operator console also reflow without horizontal scrolling (criterion 1.4.10),
  • wide tables in the help can be scrolled with a keyboard on a narrow screen (criterion 2.1.1),
  • a question input error is shown in the editor at the field and associated with it; the editor toolbar refers to an existing field even while loading (criteria 1.3.1, 3.3.1 and 4.1.2),
  • the link to information on monitoring during a test opens a dialog with the same name; emails about a returned question and about the cancellation of two-step verification have a descriptive link text instead of a bare address (criteria 2.4.4, 2.4.6 and 3.2.4).

Fixed in the version of 1 Nov 2026:

  • the projector screen of a supervised in-room exam reflows at a width of 320 px without horizontal scrolling; the address wraps and the joining code shrinks to fit the window width (criterion 1.4.10),
  • after a time extension or ending an attempt in the live monitor, focus now really returns to the live monitor heading (previously it ended up on the page heading), and the same applies after editing monitoring exceptions; after a handover code for an attempt is issued, focus stays on the page heading because the code is shown in a message at the top of the page (criterion 2.4.3).

The automated check with the axe tool (WCAG 2.1 A/AA) on 31 Oct 2026 found two minor issues (keyboard scrolling of help tables on a phone, and an editor toolbar reference to a field that was still loading); both have been fixed. The repeated check on 1 Nov 2026 covered 642 administration pages for all roles, the help and the public pages, each at a width of 1440 and 320 px, the test taker flow and the warning before sign-out. The axe tool found no violations of WCAG 2.1 criteria. The check of display at 320 px and the manual check found two issues (overflow of the projector screen of a supervised in-room exam at 320 px, and focus return in the live monitor); both have been fixed in the version of 1 Nov 2026. The public demo was being reset during the check and was not checked. However, an automated check does not reveal everything, so we keep the list of issues based on manual validation.

b) Disproportionate burden

Not applicable.

c) Content outside the scope of the applicable legislation

Not applicable.

Test time limit

If you need more time to complete a test because of a disability, ask the instructor or the exam organiser before the exam; an extension can be set in advance for a specific person and also during the test.

Sign-out after inactivity

For security reasons, the application signs out signed-in administration users after 30 minutes of inactivity, students after 60 minutes and the operator console after 15 minutes. A warning is shown 2 minutes in advance; you extend the sign-in with the “Extend sign-in” button or the Esc key. A sign-in is valid for at most 12 hours (operator console 4 hours) – this limit cannot be extended, but you are also warned about it in advance. A running test is not ended because of inactivity.

Exam monitoring

For some tests, the organisation may switch on exam monitoring, which records when the test window is left. The warning after returning to the window is shown without moving focus and a screen reader reads it; you close it with the “I understand” button or the Esc key and focus returns to where it was; full screen is only offered, and copy blocking does not restrict text selection, zoom or screen readers. However, some assistive tools (dictation, a separate magnifier, voice control) and system notifications can trigger leaving the window even if you do not interrupt the test and, in the mode with automatic submission, can submit the test. If you use such a tool, tell the instructor before you start; they can choose recording only without submission, or a test without monitoring. You can ask the exam organiser for human review of an automatic submission.

Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on 27 Oct 2026 on the basis of a self-assessment carried out by the operator of the application (ABUCO ICT s.r.o.), most recently on 1 Nov 2026 – an automated check with the axe tool and a manual check with a keyboard and the accessibility tree in the browser. A manual test with a screen reader is planned. Ukázková organizace (demo), as an obliged body under Section 2 of Act No. 99/2019 Coll., issues this statement as its own and is responsible for its publication and updating; the operator of the application prepared its draft and supplies the technical materials (the list of issues and their remedies). The statement was last reviewed on 1 Nov 2026; it is reviewed at least once a year and after every major change to the application.

Feedback and contact information

Send accessibility issues, requests for information in an accessible form and other questions to podpora@exam-test.cz (obliged body Ukázková organizace (demo)). Technical support for the application is provided by the operator at podpora@exam-test.cz or by telephone on +420 725 311 531. We will respond to your feedback without undue delay and within 30 days at the latest.

Enforcement procedure

If you do not receive a satisfactory response to your feedback from Ukázková organizace (demo), you can contact the Ministry of the Interior of the Czech Republic, which supervises compliance with Act No. 99/2019 Coll.: Ministerstvo vnitra, Nad Štolou 936/3, 170 34 Praha 7, email posta@mvcr.cz, data box ID 6bnaawp, www.mvcr.cz.
