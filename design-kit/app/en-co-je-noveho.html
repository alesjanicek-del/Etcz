What’s new

Changes in the exam-test app by version, latest first.

  1. Version 0.2.0

    28 Oct 2026Current version

    Data export. Download all of your organisation’s data (results, result reports and questions) or one person’s data. Go to Results → Export organisation and personal data.

    Record protection. Protect an official record from anonymisation and give a reason. Anonymisation now also removes the email address linked to a code, the link to the account and IP addresses.

    Test statistics now show the number of people and the number of attempts separately. Download branch and link comparisons as XLSX.

    Question history now shows the old and new text of a note, and tag names as they were at the time of the change. The test introduction is shown with its formatting.

    In-app help with search, a PDF guide for your role and printable one-page Instructions for test takers. Open help

  2. Version 0.1.0

    27 Oct 2026

    First version: a question bank with versions and approval, and tests built from question banks.

    Give a test with personal access codes, a public link, assignment to a group, a supervised in-room exam or printed paper versions.

    Results with a PDF result report that anyone can check with its verification code, plus test and question statistics.
