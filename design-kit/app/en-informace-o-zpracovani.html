Information on personal data processing

Controller: Ukázková organizace (demo) · for test takers

Unofficial translation.

The Czech version is legally binding. Informace o zpracování osobních údajů (Czech version)

At a glance

  • The controller is your organisation (Ukázková organizace (demo)). ABUCO ICT s.r.o. runs the application as the processor.
  • We process your name, your answers, your result and the technical log of the test.
  • The data are anonymised 12 months after submission. The verification code of the result report stays valid.
  • Monitoring does not use a camera, microphone or artificial intelligence. A person always decides on the result.
  • Only strictly necessary cookies, no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • You exercise your rights with the controller. You can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection.

The summary is for orientation only. The full text below is binding.

Information under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) for persons who complete a test or take an exam in the exam-test application.

Controller

The controller of the personal data is Ukázková organizace (demo).

Processor

The application is run for the controller by ABUCO ICT s.r.o., with its registered office at Třebohostická 987/5, 100 00 Praha 10, Company ID No. (IČO) 29153905, as a processor on the basis of a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR). It processes the data only on the controller’s instructions. The servers and sub-processors (hosting, sending emails) are stated in the list of sub-processors; the data are stored in the European Union. The time stamping service receives only a cryptographic hash of the records, not personal data.

What data and why

  • Identification: first name and surname and, where applicable, an email or another identifier if the exam requires them (for an anonymous test via a link, no name is required).
  • Progress and result: the test version displayed, the answers chosen, the score, the result and the exam result report with a verification code.
  • Technical log of the attempt: start time, display of questions, changes of answers, leaving the window, submission, IP address and browser type – used to demonstrate that the exam was conducted properly and to deal with objections.
  • Problem report (if you send one during the test): contact details and the text of the report – used to handle it.
  • Certificates and training acknowledgements (only for tests that issue or require them): the name of the training, the date of completion and the end of validity of the certificate, the text of the acknowledgement that you have been made familiar with the content of the training, and the time of the acknowledgement – used as evidence of training and to keep track of its validity.
  • Reminders of certificate expiry (if the controller has switched them on): the email from your account or the one entered with the access code – used to notify you that the training needs to be repeated. The list of persons whose validity is ending is also sent to the controller’s designated staff member.

Purpose: conducting, evaluating and verifiably documenting the test or exam and issuing the result report.

Where we get the data from: from you (data entered when starting the test, and your answers) or from the controller who registered you for the exam (name, identifier, email for sending the access code).

Legal basis

  • Conducting and evaluating the exam, issuing the result report and the technical log of the attempt (demonstrating that the exam was conducted properly and dealing with objections): compliance with a legal obligation and the exercise of official authority under the regulations governing the exam – Article 6(1)(c) and (e) GDPR.
  • A course or training on the basis of a contract (outside the exercise of official authority): performance of a contract – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Certificates, training acknowledgements and reminders to repeat training for employees: compliance with the employer’s legal obligation to train employees and keep records of this (for example, occupational health and safety training under the Labour Code) – Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

The specific legal regulation and purpose are stated by the controller in its information on personal data processing.

How long we keep the data

Personal data in exam results are kept for the period set by the controller: the application is set to 12 months from submission. If the controller is obliged to keep exam result reports under a special regulation or its filing and retention schedule, their retention follows that regulation or schedule. When the period expires, the data in the application are automatically anonymised: the name, identifier, link to the account, IP addresses and the stored PDF result report bearing the name are deleted, and only an anonymous statistical record and the verifiability of the result report remain. Official records that the controller is obliged to keep longer (e.g. in ongoing proceedings) may be protected by the controller before anonymisation; this is decided by the controller.

If an attempt has a certificate that is still valid, the attempt is anonymised no earlier than 30 days after the certificate expires, even if the retention period has ended earlier. Otherwise the certificate would lose the name and the reminder would not be sent. A certificate with unlimited validity does not extend retention. If you request erasure and the controller grants your request, the certificate can no longer be downloaded.

Other personal data in the application are anonymised or deleted after the following periods: the label and email of an unused access code 12 months after the end of the exam; a name in the waiting room of a supervised in-room exam that you did not start, 1 month after the last activity; contact details and text of a resolved problem report 24 months after resolution; change log records (which of the controller’s staff did what) 60 months. Application backups are kept for at most 30 days; anonymised data therefore cease to exist in backups no later than 30 days after anonymisation.

After anonymisation and after the end of the contract, the verifiability of the result report is ensured by the controller and the operator through a record without a name or identifier: the verification code, cryptographic hashes, the time stamp, the name of the test and the organisation, the date, the result and, for a certificate, the end of its validity. It is no longer possible to find out from it to whom the result report belongs. After the end of the contract, this record is kept for 5 years; after that, the result report can no longer be verified on the verification page. The controller may have this record deleted earlier or have the result removed from it.

Recipients

The data are seen by the controller’s authorised staff (instructors, administrators) within the scope of their permissions and by the operator of the application as the processor. The operator’s support technician enters the organisation only with the controller’s consent and does not open result details when doing so. Anyone who knows the verification code from the result report will see on the verification page the initials of the name (until anonymisation), the name of the test and the organisation, the date and result of the exam and, for a certificate, also the name of the training and its validity date – so share the code only with those to whom you want to prove your result. The data are not transferred to third countries or international organisations.

Automated evaluation

The test is scored automatically according to scoring rules set in advance. The consequences of the result (e.g. recognition of the exam) are decided by the controller. You have the right to express your point of view, to contest the result and to ask for it to be reviewed by a person on the controller’s side.

Your rights

You have the right of access to your personal data (including a copy of results and result reports), to rectification, to erasure (unless the controller is obliged to keep the data), to restriction of processing, to data portability (for processing based on a contract) and the right to object to processing that is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority (Article 6(1)(e) GDPR). Exercise your requests with the controller; the controller will reply within one month at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.

Obligation to provide data

Without a name (and an identifier, if the exam requires one), the exam cannot be assigned to a specific person and a result report cannot be issued. Anonymous tests via a link do not require personal data.

Exam monitoring

Only for tests for which the controller has switched monitoring on. You will learn this in advance on the test start page, which also states its exact scope, and you are reminded of it above the questions during the test. Without monitoring switched on, only leaving the window is recorded, as part of the technical log of the attempt.

  • What is recorded: the time when you left the window or tab with the test and when you returned and, where applicable, exiting full screen, attempts to copy the text of questions or paste text from the clipboard, and an answer to a question faster than the set number of seconds. Only what happens on the test page is recorded.
  • What is not recorded: camera images, microphone sound, screen content, biometric data, other applications and windows, or a fingerprint of your device. Monitoring does not use artificial intelligence and does not calculate any suspicion score; it only shows the instructor which events occurred and how many times.
  • Purpose and legal basis: to deter cheating and to document that the exam was conducted properly. The record is part of the technical log of the attempt and of the result report, and the same legal basis applies to it as to the technical log of the attempt (see Legal basis), as well as the same retention period. You can object with the controller to processing based on legitimate interest or public interest.
  • Who sees the record: the instructor and the controller’s authorised staff, in the result, in the result report and in the test statistics. Monitoring events are not shown on the public verification page or in the verification archive; for an automatically submitted attempt, the verification page states only whether the controller has assessed it and whether it recognised the result or invalidated the attempt.
  • Who decides: the events are only information for the instructor and the controller. A person always decides whether they mean anything and on any further steps.
  • Automatic submission: if the controller has set submission after repeatedly leaving the window, the test is submitted by itself, with the answers saved so far, when the stated number of times is reached, and it is evaluated in the same way as if you had submitted it. This is an automated step. You have the right to express your point of view, to contest the submission and the result, and to ask for them to be reviewed by a person on the controller’s side (Article 22(3) GDPR). Make your request to the controller, preferably right after the exam, and state why you left the window (for example, a technical problem or an assistive tool). The controller assesses the submission and either recognises the result or invalidates the attempt and allows you a replacement attempt, which does not count towards the number of attempts. The decision with its reason is recorded with the result and in the result report, and you receive it by email if you have an email with your account or access code.
  • Disability and assistive tools: some assistive tools (for example, dictation or a separate magnifier) and system notifications can trigger leaving the window even if you do not interrupt the test. If you use such a tool, tell the instructor in advance; they can set a “record only” exception for you (without warnings and submission) or “no monitoring”. For the exception, the instructor records only a general reason, for example “assistive technology”. You do not have to justify the information about the tool with a diagnosis.

Cookies and browser storage

We use only cookies and browser storage that are strictly necessary for the service to work as you request it. We therefore do not need your consent to use them. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, measurement scripts or third-party content (fonts, maps, videos).

  • Session (__Host-ETSESS): sign-in, two-step verification, access to an exam entered with a code, and form protection. Valid until the browser is closed. The sign-in ends on signing out, after inactivity (in administration after 30 minutes, for students after 60 minutes, in the operator console after 15 minutes) and no later than 12 hours after sign-in (operator console 4 hours). The server deletes other session data, for example access to an exam entered with a code, after 12 hours without activity at the latest.
  • Form protection (__Host-csrf-token_…): a one-time random value against cross-site request forgery. Valid until the browser is closed.
  • Attempt in progress (__Host-et_a_…): links a test in progress to your browser so that no one can complete it from another device. Valid for 1 day.
  • Supervised in-room exam (__Host-et_l_…): recognises your device in the waiting room of a supervised in-room exam. Valid until the browser is closed.
  • Public demo (demo_visit): only at the demo address; recognises that you are returning and takes you back to your sample branch. Contains a random visit number and the chosen role. Valid until the next nightly deletion of the demo (3:00).
  • Browser session storage (sessionStorage): during a test, temporarily holds answers that have not yet been saved to the server because of a connection outage, and notices for the test taker (exam-queue-…, exam-notice-…, exam-announced-…). For a test with exam monitoring and a full-screen offer, it also remembers that you were taking the test in full screen so that full screen can be restored on the next question (exam-fs-…, a yes/no flag only). Unsaved answers are sent as an ordinary answer save once the connection is restored; the other values remain only in your browser. In the question editor, the draft content of a question is saved here continuously so that you can restore it after being signed out for inactivity (exam-test:question-draft:…, the key contains identifiers of the organisation and your account); the draft is deleted on signing out, after 12 hours at the latest. Everything in this storage is deleted when the browser tab is closed.
  • Browser local storage (localStorage): the time of your last activity in administration, so that the warning before sign-out applies equally in all open tabs (exam-test:session-activity), and the choice to pause the automatic refresh of the live monitor (exam-test:frame-refresh-paused:…, a yes/no flag only). It contains no personal data and remains in the browser even after it is closed until you delete it; the time value is overwritten on your next activity.
