Organisation settings aren’t available in the demo

Everyone in the demo shares one sample organisation, so you cannot change its settings. In the full version, the organisation administrator sets here:

  • how questions are scored and approved,
  • email reminders before training validity ends,
  • how long results are kept, and whether two-step verification is required.
