The diagram shows the penetrating power of three types of ionising radiation. Which arrow represents alpha radiation?

  1. Aarrow 1Correct answer
  2. Barrow 2
  3. Carrow 3
  4. Dit cannot be determined from the diagram

Feedback

For a correct answer

Correct.

Alpha particles are stopped by a sheet of paper (or the outer layer of skin). Beta radiation is stopped by a few millimetres of aluminium. Gamma radiation is only attenuated by lead, not fully stopped.
